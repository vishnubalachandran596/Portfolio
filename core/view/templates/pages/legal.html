What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information {{.ContactName}}'s website ("this site") collects, why, and what choices you have. This is a plain-language policy for a personal portfolio and booking site, not a substitute for legal advice for your own situation.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to operate the site:

  • Account information: the name and email address you provide when creating an account, plus either a securely hashed password (never stored in plain text) or, if you sign in with Google, the name and email address Google shares with us (we ask Google only for "openid email profile" and never receive your Google password). If you sign up with a password, we also store a security question you choose and a securely hashed answer, used only to reset a forgotten password. An account is needed to book consultancy, take a course quiz or manage software licences; it is not needed to buy a course.
  • Consultancy bookings: the time slot you selected, an optional message, and payment status (pending/confirmed), tied to your account.
  • Course and software purchases: the email address you enter (or your account email), the fixed price charged, any promo code, and payment status (paid/failed). A course purchase made without an account also stores a random claim code that lets you attach it to an account later. We send no emails. We never see or store your card details — payment is handled entirely by Razorpay's own checkout, which runs in its own secure window and passes us only a payment confirmation.
  • Article contributions: the email address and amount you enter when optionally supporting an article, with payment status and the time it was paid, tied to your account (or a guest account created from that email). We never see or store your card details — same Razorpay checkout as courses.
  • Quizzes and certificates: your quiz attempts (answers, score, attempt count) and, once you pass, a certificate with your name, the course, your score, the date and a certificate code. Anyone who has the certificate code can open the public verification page, which shows exactly those details, so only share your code with people you want to verify it.
  • AI assistant (chatbot): the questions you type and the recent messages of that conversation are sent to NVIDIA's hosted AI service to generate an answer from this site's content. Please do not type personal or confidential information into it. We store the text of questions and answers (without your name or email) so repeat questions can be answered faster, and an anonymous random session identifier (kept in a cookie) to count a daily message limit.
  • Security logs: to block password-guessing we keep failed-attempt counters keyed to a one-way hash of your email address or IP address, never the raw value.
  • Admin account: if you're the site administrator, your email and a securely hashed password.
  • Cookies: a cookie-consent preference, your login session cookie, a per-course access token (only if you've purchased a paid course), an anonymous chat session cookie (if you use the AI assistant), and — for the administrator only — a separate admin session cookie. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Third-party processors we use, and why: Razorpay processes course, software and optional article-contribution payments (they receive your card/payment details directly; we never do), Google provides optional sign-in, NVIDIA processes AI assistant questions, Supabase hosts our database, and Vercel hosts the application. We don't use any third-party advertising or analytics trackers. Some of these providers may process data on servers outside India; we use them only to run the site and not for any other purpose.

Why we process it, and your consent

We process your personal data only for the purposes described on this page, and only with your consent or where you have voluntarily provided it to us for that purpose (for example by creating an account, booking a session, making a purchase or asking the assistant a question). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account yourself (Account → Delete my account) or writing to us; this does not affect processing that already happened, and we may keep payment records where the law requires.

How we use it

Account and booking information is used solely to authenticate you, schedule and follow up on the session you booked, grant access to courses you've purchased, and record optional article contributions. It is not sold, rented, or used for marketing without your separate consent.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (hosted by Supabase) and served by an application hosted on Vercel. Both providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

  • Account details, quiz attempts and certificates: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Booking, purchase and licence records: as long as needed to provide the service and support, and for as long as accounting and tax law require us to keep payment records.
  • Security counters: short-lived; they reset after a lockout window.
  • AI assistant question and answer text, and the daily message counter: kept only as long as they are useful for answering repeat questions and can be deleted on request.

Keeping your data safe, and breaches

Passwords and security answers are stored only as one-way hashes, connections use HTTPS, payment card details never reach us, and database tables are protected by access rules. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of personal data we hold occurs, we will tell the affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.

Your rights, including data deletion

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it, withdraw your consent, nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to, and raise a grievance. Write to us at any time{{if .ContactEmail}} at {{.ContactEmail}}{{else}} via the contact details on the Contact page{{end}}; we aim to reply within 30 days and will action verified deletion requests within that time, except for records we must keep by law. You can also delete your account yourself at any time from your account page: this immediately removes your access to every purchase (no refund), deletes your licences, quiz attempts, certificates and testimonial, and keeps only anonymous payment records (amount and date, no name or email) that tax law requires.

Grievance contact

Grievance officer: {{.ContactName}}{{if .ContactEmail}}, {{.ContactEmail}}{{end}}. If we do not resolve your complaint you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children's data

This site is not directed at children. Under Indian law a child is anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent or legal guardian's verifiable consent. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

What cookies this site uses

We keep cookie use to the minimum needed to run the site:

  • Cookie consent preference — remembers that you've seen and dismissed the cookie banner, stored in your browser.
  • Login session — an HttpOnly, secure session cookie issued when you log into your account, used to keep you signed in for booking and course access. You only receive this if you create an account.
  • Chat session — a random, anonymous identifier (HttpOnly, kept for up to a year) set only if you use the AI assistant, used to count a daily message limit. It contains no name or email.
  • Course and software access token — issued only after you've purchased a paid course or software (including as a guest), so you don't have to re-authenticate to view it.
  • Admin session — a separate HttpOnly, secure session cookie issued only when the site administrator logs in, used purely to keep that admin session authenticated.

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking your session cookie simply logs you out; it doesn't affect anyone else.
