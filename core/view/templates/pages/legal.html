What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information {{.ContactName}}'s website ("this site") collects, why, and what choices you have. This is a plain-language policy for a personal portfolio and booking site, not a substitute for legal advice for your own situation.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to operate the site:

  • Booking requests: the name, email address, and optional message you submit on the Booking page, plus the time slot you selected.
  • Admin account: if you're the site administrator, your email and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Cookies: a cookie-consent preference, and — for the administrator only — a session cookie that keeps you logged into the admin portal. See our Cookie Policy for details.

How we use it

Booking information is used solely to schedule and follow up on the session you requested. It is not sold, rented, or used for marketing without your separate consent.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (hosted by Supabase) and served by an application hosted on Vercel. Both providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Your rights, including data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you (for example, a booking you submitted) at any time{{if .ContactEmail}} by emailing {{.ContactEmail}}{{else}} via the booking page or the contact details on the About page{{end}}. Children's data

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.

What cookies this site uses

We keep cookie use to the minimum needed to run the site:

  • Cookie consent preference — remembers that you've seen and dismissed the cookie banner, stored in your browser.
  • Admin session — an HttpOnly, secure session cookie issued only when the site administrator logs in at /admin/login, used purely to keep that admin session authenticated. Visitors who never log in as admin never receive this cookie.

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking the admin session cookie has no effect on regular visitors — it only affects the ability to stay logged into the admin portal.
